Understanding credit card rewards

Cashback, points, miles, welcome bonuses and partner offers can be great value – if you understand how they work and what you give up in fees and interest.

Main types of rewards

Cashback

A percentage of your spending back as cash or statement credit. Easy to understand and compare.

Points

Flexible points you can redeem for travel, gift cards or statement credit. Value depends on the program.

Miles

Airline- or travel-focused rewards, often best when used for flights or upgrades.

How to evaluate a rewards card

1. Start with fees, not perks. Annual fee + foreign fees + potential interest cost matter more than a free airport coffee.

2. Look at your own spending pattern. A supermarket card is useless if most of your spend is travel – and vice versa.

3. Check effective reward rate. 1–2% real value on your typical spend is fine, anything clearly above that usually comes with trade-offs (fees, caps, conditions).

4. Be careful with welcome bonuses. Big intro bonuses can be great, but only if you meet the spend requirement without changing your behaviour or going into debt.

Simple reward scenarios

Scenario Reward style What to watch
Everyday spending Flat-rate cashback (e.g. 1–2%) Annual fee vs. how much you actually spend.
Frequent traveller Miles or travel points Lounge, insurance and FX fees in addition to miles.
Sign-up bonus One-time points or cashback Minimum spend requirement and first-year fee.
