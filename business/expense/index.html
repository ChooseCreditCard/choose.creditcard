Expense Cards for Employees

Expense cards let employees pay for travel, subscriptions and day-to-day costs, while the company keeps control through limits, rules and real-time monitoring.

This page is a short guide. For actual card products, use our comparison view.

What is an expense card?

Basic idea

An expense card is a card issued to an employee, linked to a business account or corporate card program, with rules set by the company.

Key benefits

  • No more personal cards for company spend
  • Automatic transaction feeds into expense tools
  • Clear separation between business and private purchases

Controls companies typically get

Limits

Per-card limits per month, per day or per transaction.

Merchant controls

Allow or block categories like hotels, airlines, fuel or online ads.

Geography & channel

Cards that only work online, in-store, or in certain countries/regions.

Expense cards vs traditional reimbursements

Aspect Expense cards Reimbursements
Cashflow for employees Company pays directly Employees use own card, wait for repayment
Control Limits & rules up front Control only after expense is submitted
Admin Automatic data feed into software Manual receipts and approvals

What to check before choosing an expense card program

  • Fees per card, per user or per transaction.
  • Integration with your accounting / ERP / expense tools.
  • Support for virtual cards for online services and subscriptions.
  • Real-time notifications and approval flows.
  • Travel insurance, FX fees and limits for frequent travellers.
