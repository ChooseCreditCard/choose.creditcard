Business & Corporate Credit Cards

Business cards help companies manage expenses, issue employee cards, earn rewards on company spending and keep personal and business finances separate.

What is a business credit card?

A business card is issued to companies (small or large) and used by employees or owners. Limits, controls and perks differ from consumer cards.

Why companies use them

  • Separate personal and business expenses
  • Centralized expense tracking
  • Easier accounting & VAT reporting
  • Higher limits for company purchases

Advanced features

  • Employee cards with limits
  • Category-based controls
  • Integrated software approvals
  • Sync with accounting platforms

Business vs personal cards

Feature Business card Personal card
Credit limit Higher, based on company spend Based on individual credit score
Employee cards Yes, with controls No
Rewards Business categories & travel General consumer categories
