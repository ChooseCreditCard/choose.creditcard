Business & freelance cards · Prototype hub

Business & freelance cards
built around how you actually work.

This sub-hub on Choose.Creditcard looks at cards aimed at companies, freelancers and side hustles. The focus is on expense controls, accounting tools and real costs – not just sign-up bonuses. Everything here is a prototype and educational only.

When this type of card makes sense

  • You run a company, freelance business or side project with recurring expenses.
  • You want to separate business and personal spending for accounting and tax.
  • You need employee cards, virtual cards or spend limits to control costs.
  • You care more about clean bookkeeping and tools than flashy consumer perks.
