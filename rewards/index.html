Credit Card Rewards Explained

Cashback, points, miles — rewards programs can be extremely valuable, but only if you understand how earning structures work. This page gives you a clear, simple overview.

Main reward types

Cashback

Simple and flexible. You get a percentage of your spending back as cash, credit, or statement credit.

Points

Flexible points systems where value depends on how you redeem — often best for travel or gift cards.

Miles

Airline or travel-linked miles, often giving highest value when redeemed for flights or upgrades.

How rewards are earned

  • Flat rate (e.g. 1%–2% on everything).
  • Category bonuses (e.g. travel, dining, groceries).
  • Rotating categories that change monthly or quarterly.
  • Intro bonuses for spending a certain amount early on.
  • Higher rates through partner merchants or apps.

Cashback vs Points vs Miles

Type Pros Cons
Cashback Simple, predictable, flexible Usually lower maximum value
Points Flexible, can give strong value Redemption values vary a lot
Miles Best for frequent travel Often tied to specific airlines

Tips to maximize rewards

  • Always pay the card in full — interest kills any reward value.
  • Choose categories where you actually spend money.
  • Use one primary rewards card instead of spreading spend across many.
  • Check whether the card has foreign fees before travel.
